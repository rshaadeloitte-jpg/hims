# Researcher Brief T1 — HMIS Definition, History, Taxonomy

**Task:** Research the definition, scope, history, and taxonomy of HMIS (Health/Hospital Management Information Systems). Write findings to `outputs/.drafts/hims-system-research-T1.md`.

## Questions to answer
1. What is an HMIS/HIMS? Give 2–4 credible definitions (WHO, academic, industry) with sources.
2. How does HMIS relate to and differ from HIS, EHR, EMR, LIS, RIS, and national/regional health information systems? Build a clean taxonomy.
3. History & evolution: paper records → departmental systems (1960s–80s) → integrated hospital information systems (1990s–2000s) → web/cloud/API era (2010s–present). Find dated milestones and credible sources.
4. Global context: WHO guidance on Health Information Systems; low/middle-income country implementations (e.g., OpenMRS, DHIS2, national HMIS programs) vs hospital-level HMIS.
5. Scale of the domain: rough market size or adoption figures if findable (with sources and year).
